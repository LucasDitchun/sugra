# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Participation in
project spaces should be safe and constructive regardless of experience, identity, background, or
viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Respect privacy, disclosure boundaries, and target authorization.
- Help make project spaces welcoming to newcomers and experienced contributors alike.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of
private information, sustained disruption, and personal attacks are not acceptable. Project spaces
must not be used to coordinate unauthorized access, abuse, or harm.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately, and protect the privacy of everyone involved.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's contact options. Include links or other
context needed to assess the incident, but do not repost sensitive material publicly. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
